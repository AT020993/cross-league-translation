## Why this exists, in plain words

Amendment 9 registered wording for two readings of each E28 difference: an interval above zero and an interval that includes zero. It did not foresee an interval below zero.

One came. On the 180 older arrivals, weighted by destination minutes, the league-aware forecast was **less** accurate than the forecast that knows only the destination: −0.129 EFF/36 (interval −0.209 to −0.048).

Amendment 9 says that a result which weakens a paper claim goes to Amir before any writing, and then into the main text. This amendment records his decision and the exact wording.

Nothing about them is stated here, and they play no part in the wording below. Which version the paper prints stays fixed by Amendment 7's permission rule.

This look is unregistered. Nothing from it enters the paper unless Amir picks it and a dated note says how it is reported.

## Where the paper says it

1. **Main text, the older-seasons subsection.** The four older-seasons readings of E28 are printed together: both differences, unweighted and weighted. The sentence with the below-zero reading says in plain words that on these arrivals the league table did not help, and that weighted by playing time it did worse than knowing the destination alone. The same sentence or the next says that this is a second reading of outcomes read once, and that it is exploratory.
2. **Limitations.** One sentence: the gain from the league table on arrivals of 2020–2025 did not carry back to the 180 arrivals of 2008–09 to 2015–16 from the Adriatic league and Israel.
3. **Scope of the positive result.** Wherever the paper states the E28 gain of the league-aware forecast on the 674, it names the seasons (2020–2025). If the abstract or the conclusion states that gain, the same paragraph states the older-seasons reading.
4. **No cause is given.** The paper does not say why the league table failed on the older arrivals. A reason may be offered only from an analysis that Amir has picked and that a dated note describes.

## If the printed version changes

If ACB's written permission arrives and the paper prints the version with ACB under Amendment 7's rule, the readings of the version without ACB **stay in the main text**, named as the arrivals from the Adriatic league and Israel. A change of printed version must not remove a reading that has already been reported.

## What does not change

* E28 stays exploratory. It licenses no statement of equivalence and changes no registered sentence.
* The frozen 2026–27 test carries the same two comparisons as registered read-outs (Amendment 8, S1 and S2). Its reading rules are untouched.
* The registered sentences of the older-seasons test (Amendment 7) stay as printed.
