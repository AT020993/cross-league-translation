## In plain words

* **A league-aware model is as good as translation, without translation.** On the 674 forecasts, a forecast that knows the player's history and which leagues he moves between, but uses no translation factor (destination intercept and slope plus penalised source-league offsets, penalty 10), has error 2.924 EFF/36. History plus translation has 2.935, and history alone 3.096. The head-to-head difference is +0.011 EFF/36 (95% interval [-0.027, +0.049]), so the two are not distinguishable at this sample size; the point estimate slightly favours the league-aware model.
* **It recovers about all of translation's gain.** League context alone recovers 1.07 of the gain translation achieves over history (bootstrap 95% interval 0.85 to 1.48; 69% of draws at or above 1). At penalty 1 the share is 1.04, at penalty 100 0.96.
* **The recipe does not matter.** Six standard ways of building the translation factor all add nothing detectable once the forecast knows both leagues. Every recipe's offsets-λ10 increment interval spans zero, the smallest Holm-adjusted p-value is 1.00, and the largest upper bound is 0.85% of the parent model's error (margin 2%).
* **Against a league-blind forecast.** The history → + translation gain ranges from 0.103 (R6) to 0.161 (R1) EFF/36, every interval above zero on pool A. R1 reproduces the submitted 0.161 [+0.077, +0.246] exactly.
* **The 2026-27 test is frozen.** 3,478 forecasts (1,739 domestic 2025-26 player-seasons × EuroLeague and EuroCup) were committed and pushed before any 2026-27 statistic was read (file sha256 `f3530c1a5ac369c0…`).

## What this means for the paper

* The E1 sentence the pre-registered rule licenses on pool A is: *A league-aware model matches translation's gain without the translation step (recovered share 1.07, 95% bootstrap interval 0.85 to 1.48).* The paper may not say the league-aware model is *more* accurate, because the C_H interval includes zero.
* The E2 null is not a property of one recipe: it holds for all 6 recipes (R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, R6).
* **No result here contradicts a submitted-abstract claim.** R1 reproduces the submitted values to 1e-9 (G0): history gain 0.161 [+0.077, +0.246]; destination slope 0.049 [-0.013, +0.111]; offsets λ1/10/100 -0.004/-0.002/+0.014.

## Limitations

* Exploratory: every 2020–2025 outcome was seen before these analyses were planned.
* The unpenalised translated feature competes against penalised offsets; this tilts toward translation and so strengthens the null.
* Intervals condition on the fitted forecasts; factor-estimation uncertainty is not propagated.
* P1 freeze: the official 2025-26 ACB field decisions are a local file outside the repository, so 2025-26 ACB source rates use the September control path (same minute repair, duplicate rule and identity quarantine) without those corrections; affected rows are counted above. ABA League 2025-26 data also stop in early January 2026, but ABA is in neither pre-registered list; it is excluded from the complete-source sensitivity by that list's definition and flagged by its last game date.
