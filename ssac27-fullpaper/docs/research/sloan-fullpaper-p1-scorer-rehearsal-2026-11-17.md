# SSAC27 frozen 2026-27 test: the scorer, its controls and its rehearsal

**Nothing in this note is a result.** Every outcome used here is made up: a frozen forecast plus random noise from a fixed seed. No 2026-27 statistic, roster or signing file was read. The real test is scored once, on 25 or 26 November 2026, by the command sequence at the end of this note.

**This note was last rendered on 24 November 2026, before the frozen test was scored.** What it says about what had been read, and about steps still to come, describes that day. It is not rendered again after the scoring; the score record and the paper are the record of the real test.

Registrations: `docs/research/sloan-full-paper-preregistration-2026-11-03.md` (the frozen test, P1), `docs/research/sloan-full-paper-preregistration-amendment-8-2026-11-16.md` (read-outs S1 to S5 and the reading rules), `docs/research/sloan-full-paper-preregistration-amendment-15-2026-11-17.md` (the published-table forecast, E31c, and the columns E32 needs) and `docs/research/sloan-full-paper-preregistration-amendment-18-2026-11-18.md` (nine readings of those rules, fixed on 18 November before any 2026-27 outcome exists; sha256 `d86fc9b79924c92629a95afc9852fc67339b23d3b2a85ece7d71abf943cd472e`). Issue: ATI-3906. This note is rendered by `scripts/report_sloan_fullpaper_p1_rehearsal.py` from the committed rehearsal records; no number is typed. It was first written on 17 November and rendered again on 18 November after Amendment 18.

## What this is, in plain words

- The forecasts for 2026-27 were frozen on 3 November. On 25 or 26 November they are compared once with what newcomers did in games through 24 November.
- The program that does the comparison is `scripts/sloan_fullpaper_prospective_score.py`. This note shows that it applies every registered control, and that the paper prints the registered sentence for every possible outcome.
- To show that without looking at real outcomes, the program was run on made-up outcome files. Each made-up file drives it into one state: scored, too few newcomers, not cleanly scorable, or refused.
- The rehearsal ran 15 made-up cases. All 15 ended in the state they were built to reach.

## Specification

| Field | Registered choice |
|---|---|
| Unit | One scored newcomer of the frozen test: player × destination competition, season 2026-27. In this note every such row is synthetic. |
| Population | The frozen test's population (3 Nov registration): v10 eligibility, continental games dated on or before 24 November 2026, files refreshed on or after 25 November. Minimum 60 newcomers. In the rehearsal: made-up players drawn from the 3,478 frozen rows, with made-up games. |
| Estimator | P-a, P-b, P-c, S1, S2: paired differences in mean absolute error, EFF/36. S3: mean of forecast minus outcome. S4: share of cross-league pairs ordered correctly. | Null | Zero for the paired differences and for S3; 50% for S4. |
| Filter | None beyond the frozen test's rules. No newcomer is added, removed or joined by hand. Every player who reaches eight games is kept in a ledger with the reason he is or is not scored. |
| Key | `player_name` × `league_dest`, the frozen file's key, joined by the v10 name key. |
| Uncertainty | P-a to P-c, S1, S2, S5 and E31c: the v10 two-way clustered 95% interval (player, destination club). S3 and S4: a bootstrap over destination clubs within competition, 2,000 draws, seed 0 (E18a and E10). Intervals are unadjusted. |

## What is fixed before scoring

- **Band half-widths.** The 80% and 95% error bands of a 2026-27 forecast are the league-aware forecast plus or minus 4.662 and 7.253 EFF/36. They are computed from the committed forecasts of the 674 arrivals of 2020-2025 and pinned in `docs/research/artifacts/sloan-fullpaper-p1-scorer-2026-11/p1_band_half_widths.json`. In November band coverage is descriptive only.
- **Scored-rows file.** `p1_scored_rows.parquet`, one row per scored newcomer. The columns E32 needs are: `player_display_name`, `league_dest`, `league_src`, `pir_per36_src`, `history_destination_slope`, `source_ridge_10`, `pir_per36_dest`, `games_dest`, `minutes_dest` (player name, competition, source league, last season's domestic rate, the destination-only forecast, the league-aware forecast, the outcome so far, games and minutes). The file holds 70 columns in all, among them every frozen forecast.
