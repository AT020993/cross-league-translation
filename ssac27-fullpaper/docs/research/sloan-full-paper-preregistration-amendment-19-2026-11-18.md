This amendment changes wording, order and placement. It adds no analysis. It allows one new number, which is arithmetic on a registered result (rule 5), and it says so wherever that number is printed.

## Why this exists, in plain words

Three readers who did not know which version was newer read the old paper and the new one. All three preferred the new one and none would yet advance it. They agreed on the reasons: it is about 7,000 words long where 5,000 is usual; its first page carries six claims; it says the table is "about 0.77 of the right size" and then gives a club no rule for using that; and its sentence about a table printed in 2015 reads as "nothing new here". Most of the first page's wording is fixed by earlier amendments, so it cannot be shortened without one more. This amendment lets the paper say less on its first page and move detail to the appendix. **No registered sentence leaves the paper, and no unfavourable reading leaves the main text.**

## What has already been seen (exposure disclosure)

* **E30 has been scored**, once (retained run `league-changes-score-01`, 18 November 2026): registered case "Clear"; slope 0.77 (95% interval 0.69 to 0.84); 0.57 (0.48 to 0.66) without Spain; 0.79 (0.68 to 0.88) with the table refitted only on earlier arrivals; 0.43 to 0.93 with the table values redrawn.
* **E31 exists**: the paper's league numbers and the 2015 table are "not distinguishable" on league changes (+0.016, interval −0.021 to 0.054) and on the 674 arrivals (0.026, interval −0.008 to 0.060).

* **Three blind mock readings of renders 38 and 39 exist** (18 November). They are opinions of simulated readers, not evidence about the result.
* **No result of the frozen 2026-27 test exists**, and no 2026-27 statistic, roster or signing file has been read. Rule 12 fixes where that test's results go before they exist.

## The rules

### 1. How the first page states the league-changes test (replaces Amendment 16, rule 3, second claim)

In the abstract, the second paragraph of the introduction and the conclusion, a Clear result whose interval lies below 1 is stated once in each place, in these words: "N players who changed domestic league moved in the direction the table implies, at about three quarters of its size (slope b, 95% interval L to U)". "About three quarters" is used only while b is at least 0.70 and below 0.80; otherwise the words are "at about b of its size". The same sentence says that the test was registered and frozen before its outcomes were read. The introduction adds the number of league changes and the lowest leave-one-league-out slope with its league.

The registered sentence of Amendment 14 is printed word for word, once, in Results, with its size reading. These plainer words claim less than the registered sentence ("moved in the direction", not "predicted what happened to"). For every other case of E30 the first page keeps the registered wording of the case.

### 5. One further reading of the slope, labelled (adds to Amendment 14, "What the paper may say")

An Equivalent gap between two leagues is the gap between their two league numbers divided by the weight the fitted equation gives a domestic rate when career history equals it (the coefficient of the domestic rate plus the coefficient of the career rate, for a EuroCup arrival). The paper may therefore print the registered slope and its interval divided by that weight: the change of a league changer's rate per EFF/36 of difference between the two league numbers. Today's values: 1.02 (0.92 to 1.12).

* **Label.** Wherever it is printed, the same sentence or the next says that it is arithmetic on the registered slope, stated after the result, and not a registered reading.
* **Where.** Results and the club section. Not the abstract, not the title.
* **Words.** "About as large as the gaps between league numbers" is used only while its interval includes 1. The paper says why the Equivalents are larger: they answer another question (which domestic line gives the same continental forecast), not what the same player produces in another league.
* **Limits.** It is not a statement about league strength, about a single pair of leagues, or about equivalence. It is printed with the lowest leave-one-league-out slope in the same section. The registered reading "about b of the size the table implies" is not withdrawn or softened by it.

### 6. Which gap to use (replaces one line of Amendment 12's "Not allowed" list)

The equation stays for EuroLeague and EuroCup arrivals from the ten leagues. The club section may say, in one short paragraph: to read a domestic line before a continental arrival, use the Equivalents as printed; when a player changes domestic league, his own rate moved on average by about the gap between the two league numbers (the reading of rule 5). The paragraph says that this is an average over the league changes of the test, read after the result, and not a statement about one pair of leagues or one player.
