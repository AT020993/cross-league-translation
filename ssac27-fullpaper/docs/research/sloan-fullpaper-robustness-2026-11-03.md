## E6. How much error is reducible

Each target's destination games are split into odd and even appearances in date order (schedule files `data/raw/<competition>/<season>/games/season_<season>_games.parquet`, 2020–2025). All 674 targets reconcile to the cohort's game count, minutes and EFF; full-season box-score rates equal the cohort rates for all four outcomes. The floor is the MAE a forecaster who knew each player's true rate would still incur: normal model, √(2/π) × SD × √(1 − reliability); direct, mean |odd − even| × √(m_odd m_even)/(m_odd + m_even); simulation, the same over 2,000 random equal-size game splits (seed 0).

## Limitations

* Every analysis here is exploratory: the 2017–2025 outcomes were seen before these analyses were planned.
* Translation enters unpenalised while source offsets are penalised. This tilts every increment toward translation, so it strengthens the nulls rather than weakening them.
* EuroLeague-only intervals are wide; a spanning interval there is a power statement, not evidence of no effect.
* The 2025 ACB field corrections were not applied in the history rebuild: they change only 2025 rows, which enter no 2017–2025 history; G3's exact equality on all 1,371 rows confirms this.
* The noise floor treats each player's season rate as fixed; the floor is a bound on what any forecast could achieve against this outcome measurement, not a forecast target.
* Exposure weights in the factor estimator stay as minutes for every outcome (only the outcome changes); for EFF per 100 possessions this is a choice, stated here.
