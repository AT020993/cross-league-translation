European League Equivalents: converter

Companion to Paper ID 47, MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference 2027. This page puts a domestic season in one of ten European leagues on one scale, and forecasts a first season in the EuroLeague or the EuroCup. Every number on it is read from the paper's committed result files when the page is built. None is typed.

1. The forecast, as one equation

EFF/36 is the efficiency rating per 36 minutes. x is last season's domestic EFF/36, in the league where the player had the most minutes. h is his career rate before that season: all earlier games on record taken together. A player with no earlier season has h = x.

forecast = 3.103 + 0.660·x + 0.092·h + league number
  −1.389 if x is above h
  −0.358 −0.092·x if the destination is the EuroLeague

The league number is the second column of the table in section 2. The calculator below uses the coefficients at full precision.
